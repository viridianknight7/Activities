How to navigate

Mouse: drag anywhere to pan. Scroll to zoom in and out around the pointer.

Touch: swipe with one finger; pinch with two fingers to zoom.

Search: type a fruit, character, affiliation or story arc. Matching entries remain highlighted, and Enter moves between them.

Jump: use Zoan, Logia and Paramecia at the top, or click the small map.

Shortcuts: 1 / 2 / 3 switch classes; + / − zoom; 0 fits everything; arrow keys pan; / searches.

Nothing is hidden in tabs or expandable cards. Every listed entry appears directly on this single canvas.
