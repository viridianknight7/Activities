# The Three Legendary Origins — V33
Release: 27 November 2026

## Vision and presentation contract
Exactly three Devil Fruit tree identities, Zoan, Logia and Paramecia. The selected visual reference is a chunky macro-chibi, bright, low-poly/faceted floating-island tree with oversized branches, foliage and spiral fruits, not intricate decoration. Keep the resting screen nearly text-free. Finger swiping switches classes, tapping the tree enters exploration, orbit/pinch works in the 3D view, and fruit data is displayed only on request.

**Essential distinction:** The polished HOME views are three class-specific still ILLUSTRATIONS. They are not rotatable models. EXPLORE mode hides the illustration and opens a separately modelled genuinely volumetric 3D tree. Do not claim the two modes have identical sculptural quality or that an illustration is interactive 3D.

Zoan: giant natural green grove with massive roots and oversized curled fruit stems; the separate 3D form also includes a teal dragon. Logia: purple tree integrating fire, lightning, storm, wind and ice; bold simple material masses. Paramecia: pink and ivory surreal blossom tree with gold orbit and portal.

## Validated local facts
The final V33 3D geometry has 8,240 Zoan, 8,432 Logia and 9,894 Paramecia triangles, with real X/Y/Z extents:
- Zoan: 14.646 × 21.690 × 9.390
- Logia: 14.282 × 20.317 × 8.415
- Paramecia: 14.150 × 21.707 × 8.584

All **146/146** inherited record sheets passed title, power and metadata field checks. The three home illustrations are distinct. The local browser test passed actual on-canvas fruit selection, mouse-wheel zoom and simulated finger swiping. Five responsive sizes had zero horizontal overflow, with no JavaScript page errors. Thirteen real screenshots were captured and packed.

**Unverified:** external GitHack preview accessibility, Android hardware WebGL, performance/framerate, physical multi-touch comfort and independent One Piece canon correctness. Screenshot automation used headless Chromium software 3D fallback. The illustrated home views are not actual 3D meshes. Unique faithful geometry for every fruit is incomplete.

## Future work
To make the **3D** trees match the illustration standard, use bespoke 3D asset modelling and GLB exports instead of endlessly stretching cylinders and domes. Start with one class at front/side/elevated/rear angles and test against greyscale silhouettes at phone thumbnail size. Only then replace the other two with equally sculpted geometry. Keep the user-selected macro-chibi large-facet art style, visible integrated class anatomy and no permanent screen text. Improve each fruit's individual shape, canon-audit catalogue metadata, and test sustained Android GPU and finger controls. Re-run all record, viewport, gesture and 13-screenshot checks before publishing the next release.

The GitHub plan and source ZIP are durable continuation references, not proof of automatic ChatGPT long-term memory storage.
