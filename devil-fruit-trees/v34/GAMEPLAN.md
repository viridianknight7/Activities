# Legendary Devil Fruit Trees V34 — Sculpted Origins
Release: 27 November 2026. Status: WORKING 3D PROTOTYPE; ARTISTIC ACCEPTANCE NOT MET.

## Design contract
Exactly three independent, unsquashed and genuinely three-dimensional macro-chibi flat-polygonal trees: Zoan, Logia and Paramecia. No A/B variants, no flat scene replacements, no permanent explanatory text. Swipe home to change class, tap to explore, drag to orbit, pinch/wheel to zoom, and tap one of the three current hanging fruits for data. The fruit-family selector is an optional narrow 56px strip, not a blocking menu.

Keep the chosen saturated game-art style, monumental floating islands, giant faceted hanging fruit with visible swirls, and distinctive physically grown class motifs. Zoan must have a great green canopy and recognisable turquoise mythical dragon with ivory/gold wing/crescent details. Logia must unite fire, storm/lightning, ice and wind *with its trunk and branches*. Paramecia must have twisting pink/ivory wood, large flowers, one gold orbit and a front-root portal. Avoid thin trunks, round broccoli, mushroom discs, prop-like attached elements, visual clutter and text panels.

The previous V33 home was polished illustrated still art; its 3D Explore scenes were much less impressive. The user asks what the actual sculptures look like without their lore. V34 displays the ACTUAL rotatable 3D model from the first frame. No image is misrepresented as a model.

## Explicit FAILED artistic acceptance gates
The user wants actual legendary trees worthy of creating Devil Fruits, judged at face value without labels. V34 is not yet there: Zoan's canopy/dragon remains mechanical, Logia reads like attached elemental sectors and Paramecia petals/orbit read as generic geometry. The islands also remain too toy-like. Conservative assistant estimate: 15–20% current 3D visual match (not user approval), around 90% feature completeness. Do not claim a vast or finished artistic improvement because code tests pass.
