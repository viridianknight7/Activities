# Legendary Devil Fruit Trees V32 — Crown Rebuild
27 November 2026 | Permanent development, visual and verification plan

## Face-value review
V31 did NOT look like legendary Devil Fruit trees. Zoan looked like an enormous broccoli-shaped ornamental tree, Logia like grey cloud foliage on a post, and Paramecia like pink candy floss with a decorative ring. At side and rear angles the limb architecture was hidden and class identities disappeared. Passing tests cannot rescue this artwork.

## User's binding requirements
Exactly THREE genuinely volumetric 3D macro-chibi, flat-polygonal trees: Zoan, Logia and Paramecia. No A/B forms, no six competing models, no flattened billboards, squashed crowns, thin twigs, sphere piles, mushroom tops, detached stickers or excessive decoration. Ancient giant anatomy must read without names or lore: thick flared buttress roots, tapered gnarled trunk, large physically joined boughs, deep crowns and negative space. Preserve saturated colour, islands and large Devil Fruits.

Class motifs to keep and properly integrate: Zoan as a majestic green beast grove with turquoise dragon, attached ivory feathers and a restrained crest; Logia as a dark primal tree growing fire, storm/lightning, ice and wind from the same wood; Paramecia as a pink-and-ivory spiral organism with thick blossoms, helix, lower portal and just one gold orbit.

At rest the screen has NO visible explanatory text. Finger swipe changes class, tap explores, dragging orbits, pinch or mousewheel zooms; tapping an actual hanging fruit opens its data. Family information appears only when deliberately requested in a narrow 56px horizontal list. Three hanging fruits per page. Retain 146 fruit records and 19 families.

## Verified local QA
146/146 records, 19 families, 44/12/90 counts; all eight details fields. Three genuine triangle meshes: Zoan 33,013 triangles, Logia 25,715, Paramecia 35,903. Measured XYZ bounds are recorded in the downloadable QA_REPORT.json. No JavaScript page errors or horizontal overflow across the five sizes. In-world fruit picking, horizontal swipe, orbit drag, wheel zoom, family selection, search and previous/next details pass. Synthetic pinch changed camera distance 39.40 to 37.88. Family bar is 56 px high. 20 canonical screenshot states plus nine detail-focused diagnostic captures were created.

Headless Chrome used the Canvas2D software projection of the SAME true 3D triangle meshes because hardware WebGL was disabled in the test environment. Android WebGL performance, sustained FPS, real multi-touch comfort and independent One Piece canon accuracy remain unverified; 146 individually faithful fruit sculptures are also not complete.

## Remaining artistic failings and correct future method
Visual acceptance is NOT MET. Zoan still resembles an elaborate ordinary fantasy tree, its dragon too small. Logia's elemental masses are too clearly separated. Paramecia's petal clusters remain angular and busy. V32 estimated vision match ~25%, functional completeness ~88%, both subjective and not user approval.

STOP endless additions of procedural primitives. First create a bespoke hero Zoan sculpt with meaningful trunk-root junctions, sculpted canopies and a dragon defining its silhouette, using an editable 3D authoring/GLB pipeline. Obtain approval from ACTUAL five-angle interactive renders before repeating this level of authoring for Logia and Paramecia. Independently canon-audit lore, sculpt each distinctive fruit, profile actual Android WebGL and repeat all 29 screenshots, 146 records, gestures and five viewports. Never claim that a successful programmatic audit proves legendary artistic quality.

This GitHub plan is the durable future continuation record; it is not proof that ChatGPT long-term memory has been updated.
