# Legendary Devil Fruit Trees: V35–V37 three-pass modelling batch
Date: 27 November 2026. This plan is a durable handoff, NOT a claim of visual acceptance.

## Binding vision
Three truly volumetric low-poly macro-chibi game-art trees: Zoan, Logia and Paramecia. No A/B alternates, still-image substitutes, text-heavy default screen, mushroom crowns, narrow Sudowoodo trunks or disconnected decorative stickers. Keep huge rooted anatomy, joined limbs, large facets, genuinely deep crowns, the floating island and distinct class-specific features. Keep home screen free of permanent explanatory text; swipe tree classes, tap to explore, drag to orbit, pinch/wheel to zoom, tap current 3D fruit for data, and use a 56px optional family selector.

## Iterations completed
1. **V35 Worldroot:** broad tapered trunks, eight anchored buttresses, seven 3D boughs, independent closed irregular faceted crowns and unique class bases. Interactive website generated and tested.
2. **V36 Living Legends:** much larger 3D turquoise dragon with horns/wings, fire-and-storm-with-ice features, broad rounded-end 3D lotus petal designs, one spatial orbit, lower portal and XYZ shape correction. Camera and 3D fruit attachment positions adjusted together.
3. **V37 Mythic Convergence:** shorter/wider/deeper trees, recomputed per-triangle normals, clearer Zoan moon/gold accents, Logia elemental bark veins and dimensional flower centres. Rejected an earlier spiky Paramecia petal experiment and replaced it with broad solid petal contours.

The three versions use the same **146 inherited Devil Fruit records** (44 Zoan, 12 Logia, 90 Paramecia) across **19 groups**, three active hanging fruits per family page. Eight stored metadata fields are available on each record. Canon has not been independently audited.

## Important limitations and next stage
Visual acceptance is STILL NOT MET. V37 improves structural width/depth, but the dragon and Logia storm/elemental tree remain mechanically assembled. Stop piling on generic tubes, cones and primitive crowns. Author a single bespoke Zoan hero mesh in an actual 3D model editor (editable Blender scene -> GLB) with joined bark-root-branch forms, expressive dragon head/body and intentionally sculpted polygonal foliage. Obtain user approval from the **actual interactive** front/oblique/side/elevated/rear renders before extending that standard to Logia and Paramecia. Then model visually faithful individual Devil Fruits, independently fact-check canon, profile real Android WebGL, multi-touch and frame rate, and repeat the 23-screenshot / 146-record / 5-viewport QA gate before any further publication.
