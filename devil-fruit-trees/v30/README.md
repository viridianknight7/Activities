# Legendary Devil Fruit Trees V30

Exactly three interactive, genuinely volumetric low-poly Devil Fruit tree sculptures: Zoan, Logia and Paramecia. The browser homepage intentionally has **no visible text**, class selector, instruction text or blocking menu. The island and sculptures fill the scene.

Open [the standalone webpage](index.html), or try the browser preview at https://raw.githack.com/viridianknight7/Activities/main/devil-fruit-trees/v30/index.html

**Navigation:** finger-swipe sideways to switch the three trees, tap a tree to explore, tap one of three hanging 3D fruits to open its data, drag to orbit and pinch to zoom. In explore mode the small icon bar can change fruit pages or open a narrow horizontal family picker. The top-right magnifier opens search only when requested. Desktop drag, wheel and arrow-key navigation are supported.

The offline HTML includes the full original 3D mesh/renderer implementation and the inherited 146 Devil Fruit records: 44 Zoan, 12 Logia and 90 Paramecia, across 19 groups. The records are retained from earlier releases, not independently canon verified.

Read [GAMEPLAN.md](GAMEPLAN.md) for the visual contract, design rejection criteria, ordered work and unsolved sculptural quality gates, and [QA_REPORT.json](QA_REPORT.json) for verified run-time results. The complete downloadable ZIP in the originating ChatGPT conversation contains editable art and build scripts, the catalogue JSON, full QA and 17 actual 390×731 browser screenshots.

**This is a tested interactive prototype, not artistically approved as a final legendary design.** The screenshot environment used the Canvas2D software 3D renderer; real Android WebGL/GPU performance and individually faithful 146 fruit sculptures remain unverified.
