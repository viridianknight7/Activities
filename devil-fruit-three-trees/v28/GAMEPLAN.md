Continue **only from V27** in `devil-fruit-three-trees/v27` (verified HTML Git blob `9148b3fb80c026ae491367aa526d21cd67f81629`). No later branch/project or alternative design. ## Artistic constraints
Prioritise distinct *physically volumetric*, macro-chibi low-poly sculptural anatomy and cohesive organic forms; reject stacked flat canopy plates, cones posed as fire, identical cloud balls, detached decorative elements, repeated generic islands, dense grape-like fruit bunches, and hollow geometric visual clutter. Zoan should feel like an animal-shaped ancient forest, with muscular roots, antlers/ears and a dense radial crown with visible front-back supports. Logia should feel like a single storm/fire/ice/water spirit whose natural forces become limbs and canopy, not attached ornaments. Paramecia should feature intertwined impossible trunks, multiple thick 3D flowers in different orientations, luminous portals and generous negative space. Preserve low-poly facet edges and saturated warm adventure colours. Do not flatten geometry to meet the viewport.

## Diagnostic screenshot contract
Capture 3 straight-on portraits (unique silhouettes), 3 rear/side/high portraits deliberately exposing geometry and island/waterfalls, and four *variable, not copy-pasted* inspection templates selected for deficits: (7) tree-family low orbit and bough attachment, (8) a populated family from a strong sideways view checking tap targets and overlapping stems, (9) a distinctive revealed fruit from an oblique view including legible eight-field data, (10) a sealed fruit from an opposing angle checking the chains and lock from behind. All at 390×844 after camera movement settles, plus desktop 1440×900 tree views. Each shot receives a named defect hypothesis; revise from screenshots rather than judging code volume.

## V28 observed results and continuation rules

Tested **390×844** and **1440×900** software-WebGL Chromium scenes. All 146 historical fruit records, all 19 groups, eight fact fields per fruit, primary navigation, swipe, slow orbit, wheel and simulated pinch were exercised without uncaught runtime errors. Tree geometry is Zoan X54.55 / Y72.55 / Z48.04; Logia X45.22 / Y69.48 / Z40.88; Paramecia X53.20 / Y83.18 / Z43.33; depth/width ratios 0.88, 0.90 and 0.81. The scene contains 7,749, 8,447 and 31,578 mesh triangles respectively.

**Fixes made after inspecting actual renders:** Logia's fire surfaces appeared as narrow spikes because the blade widths were facing away from the front camera. Reoriented the blade basis to expose broad warm-orange physical folds and added connected smaller flame sections. The highest Paramecia blossom looked disconnected; physically extended its branch into the blossom and added a 3-D sepal. The Zoan crown remained shallow; broadened and thickened its main radial lobes. New crowns clipped the portrait sides, so stage-zero framing was pulled back and recentered without flattening geometry. Deleted the decorative “STYLISED APPEARANCE” caption on known-look fruit detail scenes; retained the informative unknown-appearance label on sealed entries.

**Future work not yet completed:** Zoan leaves still read as procedural clusters rather than fully hand-sculpted organic foliage. Logia clouds could flow more convincingly into fire and ice. Paramecia petal surfaces could be more complex and less repetitive. The waterfalls are joined to the island rim geometrically but can seem detached from high rear viewpoints. The original 23 marked-illustrated fruit models are not audited against manga/official visual references; 123 remain sealed. No independently verified physical Android FPS, memory, battery or CDN delivery. Retain **finger-first, data-only** interface and diagnose with six distinct tree orbits plus four different, useful fruit/family views on every future release.

Do not call this version visually complete merely because its Z dimensions pass; continue using side and rear screenshots to evaluate *apparent* depth. Preserve the approved V27/V28 art language and project data.
