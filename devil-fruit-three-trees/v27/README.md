# Legendary Devil Fruit Trees V27

**V27 builds exclusively on V26.** It retains all 146 historical Devil Fruit records, all 19 family groups, all four continuous navigation stages and the finger-first, text-minimal interface. There is one unique 3D tree for each of Zoan, Logia and Paramecia, no variant selector.

The work focuses on visual construction rather than adding interface controls. Zoan uses connected sculptural bark and living antlers under irregular deep-tiered foliage. Logia forms a combined basalt/storm/flame/water organism with curling currents. Paramecia has a three-strand trunk, roses facing different spatial directions and oblique rings. The three islands have separate material palettes and geology. Fruit groups retain their physical hanging meshes. The 123 catalogue entries whose visual appearances were previously not verified remain represented by sealed fruit models. The 23 others are stylised interpretations, not canon-accurate replicas.

Tap a tree, family and fruit to enter deeper; swipe horizontally to cycle items, slowly drag to rotate and pinch to zoom. Only contextual names, compact icon navigation and fruit data are visible. The full fruit inspection view shows the eight inherited data fields.

Build locally with `python build.py`; this assembles `index.html` from `template.html`, `geometry_engine_from_v23.js`, `geometry25.js`, `geometry26.js`, `geometry27.js`, `app.js`, `data.json` and `styles.css`. `qa_v27.py` reproduces the ten intentional fault-finding views and a desktop/mobility test with Chromium software WebGL; `QA_V27.json` records the exact tests and dimensions.
