# Devil Fruit World V26 — Permanent development and continuation plan

## Scope and artistic direction

**Provenance:** V26 starts from V25 alone. Keep the 146 historical records (44 Zoan, 12 Logia, 90 Paramecia), 19 original families (12/3/4), the same continuous one-page WebGL world, and exactly **one** tree design per class.

**Visual identity:** very large chibi proportions, rich One Piece-inspired colour, broad flat facets, true unsquashed X/Y/Z sculpture, rounded but deliberately angular surfaces, floating island geology, engraved spiral inlays, and real hanging Devil Fruit family clusters. Zoan: living, animal-inspired Beast Grove with thick roots, organic antlers and layered green biological growth. Logia: natural forces literally form the storm/fire/ice/water/basalt tree. Paramecia: interwoven magenta trunks, spatially impossible gold and aqua rings, oversized rose/petal formations, airborne fragments.

**Hard user rule:** remove all unnecessary UI text. Display only current class/family/fruit names and actual Devil Fruit data. No hero headline, description, instructions, tutorial overlays, repeating counts, rectangular navigation cards, search, text modal, or decorative interface controls. Finger-first: tap 3D objects to enter, swipe to cycle, drag slowly to orbit, pinch to zoom, with small icon-only back and optional arrows. Do not switch to a different visual style or add technology merely because it is fashionable.
